# CE-CERT / Clean Transportation

Work from my role as R&D Engineer II at UC Riverside's Center for Environmental Research and
Technology (CE-CERT).

## Featured writeups

- **[OSAR Field Leak-Testing Program](OSAR_Leak_Testing_Program.md)** — field project
  management for a CARB-funded (contract 23RD009) alternative-fuel leak-detection program
  across multiple partner fleets (OCTA, RTA, Caltrans, Sunbelt Rentals), including hands-on
  compressed-gas and hydrogen-refueling safety work.
- **[ME Totalizer Dial-Verification Pipeline](ME_Totalizer_Dial_Verification.md)** — built and
  led a human-verification methodology (977 of 4,889 dial reads checked, 98% confidence, <2%
  margin of error) after two AI-vision approaches produced confident but systematically wrong
  readings.
